Frequently asked

Cron expression questions

What do the five fields in a cron expression mean?

In order, they represent minute, hour, day of month, month, and day of week. A common mistake is confusing the order of day-of-month and day-of-week, since both accept overlapping ranges of values.

What is the difference between * and ? in some cron systems?

Standard cron only uses *, meaning every value. Some schedulers like Quartz add ? to mean no specific value, used to avoid conflicts when both day-of-month and day-of-week are set to specific values.

What time zone do the upcoming run previews use?

Previews are calculated using your browser's local time zone. Production schedulers often run in UTC or a server-configured time zone, so double-check the target environment before deploying.
