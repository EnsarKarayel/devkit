Privacy Policy

Formalint tools run in your browser. Text pasted into the formatter is processed locally and is not sent to a server by this static version.

Tool Input

JSON, XML, YAML, SQL, Python snippets and other tool input entered into Formalint are handled by client-side JavaScript. Formalint does not currently provide accounts, server-side storage, upload processing, or sync features.

Advertising and Cookies

Formalint may display advertising after Google AdSense is enabled. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Users can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

If additional advertising partners are added, this page should be updated with links to the relevant vendor policies and opt-out controls, including industry opt-out resources such as aboutads.info where appropriate.

Analytics

Formalint uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate page views, tool usage patterns and basic device/browser information. Analytics storage is disabled until a visitor accepts optional measurement in the privacy preferences banner.

Analytics data is used to improve the tools, prioritize new pages and monitor site performance. Formalint does not send pasted tool input to Google Analytics.

Security Reminder

Do not paste secrets, production credentials, private keys, authorization tokens, regulated personal data, or confidential customer data into any online tool unless your organization has approved that workflow.

Contact

For security, privacy or product questions, contact Formalint through the contact page.
